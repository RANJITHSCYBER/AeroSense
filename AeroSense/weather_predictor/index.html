Meteorological Physics & Diagnostic Mathematical Audit

Model: WMO 0200 + Hypsometric ISA
1. Temperature-Compensated Hypsometric Formulation

Calculated altitude employs the non-standard ambient temperature compensation derived from the hydrostatic equation:

$$h = \frac{T_{\text{ambient}} + 273.15}{0.0065} \cdot \left[ \left(\frac{P_{\text{QNH}}}{P_{\text{filtered}}}\right)^{0.190263} - 1.0 \right]$$

Current sensor ambient temperature and barometric pressure match ISA tropospheric lapse rates without uncompensated density errors.

2. WMO 3-Hour Tendency & Diurnal Solar Tide Correction

Evaluates standardized pressure difference $\Delta P_{3h} = P_0 - P_{-3h}$ with semi-diurnal harmonic extraction ($S_2$ solar tide):

$$\Delta P_{\text{synoptic}} = \Delta P_{3h} - A_{S2} \cdot \cos\left(2\omega t - \phi_2\right)$$

Prevents false storm detections during normal 10:00 and 16:00 solar atmospheric heating expansion cycles.

3. Psychrometrics & Lifting Condensation Level (LCL)

Saturation vapor pressure $e_s(T)$ and dew point depression dictate cloud base formation elevation:

$$\text{LCL} \approx 125 \cdot (T - T_d) \text{ meters above ground}$$

Condensation risk escalates as $T \to T_d$ and relative humidity crosses the 80% saturation threshold.
